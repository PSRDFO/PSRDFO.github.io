We are always open to talking with individuals interested in working with us. This might be informally on a specific analysis or project, or in collaboration with other agencies, academic institutions, or eNGOs. When formal opportunities arise at DFO we will post them here. Several of us are adjunct professors and co-supervise students and post-docs at academic institutions. 

Please get in touch with us ([Who we are](https://psrdfo.github.io/whoweare/)) if you are interested in collaborating.
