**Twenty years of Canada’s Wild Salmon Policy: reflections on the past and a guide for the future**. 2025 marked the 20th anniversary of [Canada's Policy for the Conservation of Wild Pacific Salmon](https://www.pac.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/fm-gp/salmon-saumon/wsp-pss/policy-politique-eng.html) (aka the Wild Salmon Policy or WSP). To mark the anniversary Brendan and [Michael Price](http://www.michaelprice.org/) ([SkeenaWild](https://skeenawild.org/) and Simon Fraser University) organized a special collection of papers that reflected on past successes and challenges and reimagined how the Policy might evolve and adapt to deliver on its promise to restore and maintain healthy and diverse salmon populations for generations to come. The special collection, published in the Canadian Journal of Fisheries and Aquatic Sciences, [can be found here](https://cdnsciencepub.com/topic/wild-salmon-policy) and a synthesis of the papers and recommendations stemming from an April 2026 workshop will be posted here when published. 

**Science to support rebuilding of Yukon Chinook salmon**. We recently completed an [assessment of Canadian-origin Yukon River Chinook salmon](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2026/mpo-dfo/fs70-6/Fs70-6-2026-008-eng.pdf), a culturally and ecologically important stock complex that has declined by approximately 87% since the 1980s and is now at some of the [lowest abundances ever recorded](https://yukon-news.com/2026/07/08/low-returns-continue-to-threaten-yukon-river-chinook/). Our assessment reviewed [key drivers of decline](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/feature-story/shedding-new-light-yukon-river-chinook-salmon-declines), including climate-related impacts, poor marine survival, and reduced reproductive potential, and reconstructed fine scale Conservation Unit abundances over the past 40 years. We then used this information to   evaluate alternative harvest and rebuilding strategies to support recovery, including the unprecedented moratorium on directed fishing for one salmon generation announced by the [Alaska and Canada](https://www.pac.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/fm-gp/salmon-saumon/yukon-chinook-agreement-quinnat-accord/index-eng.html) in 2024. This work helps to strengthen the science based foundation of information upon which fishery and rebuilding actions can be taken moving forward now that Yukon Chinook are formally subject to [Fishery (General) Regulations](https://gazette.gc.ca/rp-pr/p2/2026/2026-07-01/html/sor-dors143-eng.html) under the *Fisheries Act*.
